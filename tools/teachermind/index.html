    How to play

    1. Read the story. Press “Continue” to move forward.
    2. You decide what the characters do. Each character has 0 to 5 stars.
    3. A star appears when a character starts to see a new possibility. There are no wrong answers.

    💡 Underlined words are tappable. Tap one to see a simple explanation.

    Your progress is saved in this browser.
